THE COMPLETE METHOD

Every formula, step by step

This is the exact method used by GooberStats. You can follow the symbols, insert the published numbers, and check our calculation yourself.

1

Monthly Winrate

This is the simple monthly result. It includes every game we know for the selected month.

Monthly WR = 100 × Monthly Wins / Monthly Games

Monthly Wins = wins during the month · Monthly Games = games during the month.

2

Expected Winrate before an hour

Expected WR is our estimate of a player’s strength before that hour is played. The hour being rated can never change its own expectation.

Historical weight = min(300 − Grecent, Ghistory) × 2−age / 365Expected WR = (Wrecent + Historical weight × WRhistory + Prior games × WRpopulation) / (Grecent + Historical weight + Prior games)Expected WR is limited to the range 2%–98%.
  • Grecent / Wrecent: up to the previous 300 observed games and wins.
  • Ghistory / WRhistory: older profile evidence for returning players.
  • age: days since that historical profile. Its weight halves every 365 days.
  • Prior games: 40 for a known returning player, otherwise 100.
  • WRpopulation: the median historical winrate of established observed players.
3

Raw Hourly Lobby Effect

Each player is weighted by how many games they actually played. Five strong players playing once do not have the same influence as five strong players playing ten games each.

qi(δ) = logistic(logit(Expected WRi) + δ)Choose δ so that: Σ Gamesi × qi(δ) = Actual Wins in the hourInformation I = Σ Gamesi × qi(δ) × (1 − qi(δ))

A negative δ means the observed players won less often than their baselines predicted: the hour appears harder. A positive value means it appears easier.

4

Confidence and smoothing

A single noisy hour should not be trusted as much as a well-observed hour. We pull it toward the stable hour-of-day prior. More games and useful information give the real snapshot more influence.

Weight λ = τ² / (τ² + 1 / (8 × I))Smoothed effect s = Hour prior + λ × (Raw effect δ − Hour prior)

τ² is the estimated real variance between hours. The multiplier 8 controls PPS smoothing. Confidence labels use I: very low below 10, low from 10, medium from 25, and high from 50.

5

Lobby Bonus

The easiest stable hour-of-day is the zero-bonus reference. A harder smoothed effect produces an exponential bonus.

Lobby Bonus% = max(0, (eEasiest reference − s − 1) × 100)Lobby Factor = 1 + Lobby Bonus% / 100

This is why an exceptional event hour can receive a very large bonus. It still affects a player only through the wins actually earned during that hour.

6

Public Performance Score (PPS)

Every observed win is multiplied by its hour’s Lobby Factor. The result is divided by all of that player’s observed games.

Weighted Winst = Winst × Lobby FactortPPS = 1000 × Σ Weighted Winst / Σ Gamest

Important: PPS is not always exactly Monthly WR × average Lobby Factor × 10. The precise formula knows in which hours the wins happened. A player needs at least 30 rated games before a monthly PPS is published.

READ THIS BEFORE COMPARING PLAYERS

PPS is an estimate, not a skill detector

We could measure performance perfectly only if we had every individual lobby and every player in it. We do not. GooberStats sees public profile changes during the hours we successfully observe, with approximately — coverage. Unknown profiles and missing hours create uncertainty.

Play styles are different

Some Goobers maximize safe wins. Others experiment, joke around, help friends, or take risks. A lower PPS player can still have more mechanical skill than a higher PPS player.

Fair play cannot be measured

Friends can help someone win. Alt accounts can smurf or sabotage another player. Public profile totals do not tell us why a win or loss happened, so PPS cannot reliably detect or correct this.

Observed does not mean complete

We do not know every profile, every bot, or the exact players sharing each real lobby. “Hourly Lobby Factor” rates the public field observed during that hour—not one specific in-game lobby.

Performance is not permanent skill

PPS answers: “How valuable were the wins we observed in this period?” It does not answer: “Who would always win a fair match?” Treat it as a useful approximation, never as proof.
